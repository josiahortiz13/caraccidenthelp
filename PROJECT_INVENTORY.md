## STOP items (fix before any ad spend)

### 1. `api/crash-alert.js` + `.github/workflows/crash-alert.yml` — turn this off now
Every 5 minutes, GitHub Actions hits `/api/crash-alert`. It reads Houston TranStar's live incident feed and texts every number in `PARTNER_PHONES`:
"If you respond to this scene, hand the driver our card. Free legal case review: (346) 860-1929."

That asks tow drivers (or anyone on the list) to solicit legal work from crash victims at the scene. Texas Penal Code §38.12(a)(2) and (a)(4) make soliciting employment for a lawyer, in person or through someone else, a third-degree felony. §38.12(b)(3) also exposes any attorney who knowingly accepts those cases. It also breaks the agent spec's own rule against using crash data to reach victims.

**Action:** disable the "Crash Alert" workflow in GitHub (Actions → Crash Alert → Disable workflow), remove `PARTNER_PHONES` and `CRON_SECRET` in Vercel, and delete or rewrite the endpoint. This session can't push to your repo, so this one is on you. It takes 30 seconds.

### 2. Homepage claims that are not real
`index.html` shows content that a new referral service can't back up:
- "Recent Texas Settlements": 8 cards with dollar amounts ($1.4M Houston, $875K Dallas, …)
- "5.0 · Based on 47 Google reviews" plus 7 written reviews, and `ratingValue: 5.0` in the page's structured data
- "Recovered for Clients" / "Cases Won" stat cards, "We Fight. You Get Paid.", "3 Steps to Get Paid"
- "Licensed Texas Attorneys" and "State Bar of Texas" trust badges on a site that is not a law firm

Fabricated reviews and results are illegal under the FTC's 2024 fake-review rule, break Texas attorney-advertising rules for the firm, and will get Meta and Google ads rejected. **Action:** remove all of it. The redesign concept already replaces it with honest content (checklist, deadline counter, how-it-works).
