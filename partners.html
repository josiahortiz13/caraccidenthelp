Questions

Partner FAQ

Everything you need to know before signing up.

How do I actually refer someone?

Give them our number (346) 860-1929, share crashhelptx.com, or text us their name and number directly from your partner contact. We track it back to you and handle everything from there.

When and how do I get paid?

Payouts are processed weekly. We'll confirm your preferred payment method during your onboarding call — options include Zelle, check, or direct deposit.

What makes a referral "qualified"?

The person you refer must have been involved in a car accident in Texas and be reachable for a free case review. If the case proceeds, it becomes a signed case payout.

Is there a minimum number of referrals to get paid?

No minimum. Your first referral that qualifies gets paid. There's no lock-in, no quota, and no monthly commitment.

Is this legal? Am I soliciting clients?

You're connecting accident victims with a free referral service — not an attorney. CrashHelpTX is a legal referral intermediary. We handle the legal compliance. You're just passing along a number.

Can I refer someone who was hurt a while ago?

Yes. Texas has a 2-year statute of limitations for personal injury claims. As long as the accident was within that window, they may still have a case worth reviewing.
