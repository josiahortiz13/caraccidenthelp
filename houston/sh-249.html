Good to know

Does it cost anything to call?

No. Calling CrashHelp TX is free to you, 24/7.

Is CrashHelp TX a law firm?

No. CrashHelp TX is not a law firm and does not give legal advice. Contacting us does not create an attorney-client relationship.

How long do I have?

Texas generally gives two years to file most injury claims. The exact deadline depends on the facts, so it's worth asking an attorney sooner rather than later.

What if I think the crash was partly my fault?

Fault isn't always clear. An attorney can explain how Texas fault rules work for your situation. We don't make that call.
