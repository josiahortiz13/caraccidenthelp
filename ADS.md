# Meta Ad Copy: Houston Corridor Pages (SH-249, Airline Dr, Beechnut)

15 Meta ads (5 variations x 3 landing pages), each in English and Houston Spanish. Each one points to its corridor page: a 3-question quiz (when it happened, whether anyone was hurt, whose fault it was), then name and phone with consent, plus a 24/7 call button.

**Compliance notes (apply to every ad):**
- Every primary text includes "CrashHelp TX is not a law firm." / "CrashHelp TX no es un bufete de abogados."
- No money, compensation, settlement or outcome promises. No paid-referral angles. No testimonials, reviews, ratings or statistics, including crash counts or crash-record data (TranStar or any other) for these roads.
- No graphic injury language. Nothing that states the viewer's situation or suggests we know about their crash. Ads use "If you've been in a crash..." wording.
- The only legal fact used is that Texas *generally* allows 2 years to file most injury claims. Nothing says or suggests CrashHelp TX is a law firm or an attorney, and no ad promises a "free case review." Ads say "free to call" or "free to you."
- Headline character counts are in parentheses (Meta limit target: under 40).
- CTA buttons are standard Meta buttons. Spanish ads use the same button, and Meta shows it in the viewer's language automatically (e.g. Call Now → "Llamar ahora", Learn More → "Más información", Contact Us → "Contáctanos").
- **Approval required:** This copy may count as lawyer advertising for the partner firm. Per LEGAL_REVIEW.md item 13, the partner firm's attorney must approve it before it runs, and it may also need a responsible-attorney name or Texas Bar Advertising Review filing.

UTM template: `?utm_source=meta&utm_medium=paid&utm_campaign=<slug>&utm_content=v1..v5`
